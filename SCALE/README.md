# S.C.A.L.E

You are a test subject (SUBJ-7355608) in the S.C.A.L.E. (Spatial Calibration and Logistics Engine) testing facility run by Glass Research Inc. You are given the S.C.A.L.E. Device, a state-of-the-art tool that allows you to shrink and expand objects at will. Glass Research scientists have developed a series of puzzles to both test your cognitive function and the overall reliability of the device. 

Final game will feature a number of testing chambers with unique puzzles.

## Controls

Move - WASD

Look - Mouse

Shrink/Expand - Q/E

Pickup/Drop - F


## Objective

Your objective is to find your way out of the test chamber using the S.C.A.L.E. tool and the cubes around the demo area.
